I am an atmospheric and climate scientist broadly interested in the dynamics of weather and their connections to climate variability and change. I seek process-level understanding using observation and models of varying complexity.

I am currently a Postdoctoral Research Fellow in the Department of Earth and Environmental Sciences and the Schiller Institute for Integrated Science and Society at Boston College. My current research focuses on tropical atmospheric dynamics, particularly mesoscale convective systems (MCSs) and squall lines.

I received my Ph.D. in Earth and Atmospheric Sciences from Georgia Institute of Technology in 2025, back when my research focused on midlatitude atmospheric dynamics. I explored the multiscale dynamics underlying the variability of MCSs in the United States and hydrological extremes in other extratropical regions.
