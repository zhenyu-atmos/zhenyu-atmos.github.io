Zhenyu You

Hi! I’m a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at Boston College, working with Yi Ming in the Department of Earth and Environmental Sciences and the Schiller Institute for Integrated Science and Society.

I study organized convection, large-scale circulation, and regional precipitation extremes. I use observations and models to understand the physical mechanisms connecting storms with their dynamical background.

I received my Ph.D. in Earth and Atmospheric Sciences from Georgia Tech in 2025, advised by Yi Deng.
